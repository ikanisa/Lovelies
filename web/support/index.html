Lovelies support

Here to help.

Get account help, report an incorrect match or raise a privacy concern.

Contact support

Email info@ikanisa.com. You can contact us without installing or signing in to the app.

Open a support email ↗

Describe what happened. If you have them, include an account or existing request ID, the app version and device model. Support checks ownership before disclosing or changing private account data. Email alone does not establish account ownership.

Protect your private information

Do not email photos, selfies, biometric material, device keys, recovery secrets or payment-card details. Support does not ask you to send a private recovery backup in email.

Incorrect matches or unwanted content

Use the app’s report and block controls where available. A possible match is a suggestion, not proof of identity or permission to share. If you cannot use the app, describe the issue by email with a current report or request ID if known; do not attach private photos or face data.

Account deletion or an export

Use the app’s privacy settings to request an export or track a deletion request. If the app is inaccessible, the account-deletion page explains the email route and ownership check. A saved request is separate from verified cleanup completion.

Store purchases

Use the purchasing Apple or Google account to manage subscription renewal. Restore purchases in Lovelies to ask the backend to verify current entitlement. Pending, expired or revoked purchases do not grant original-photo access. Deleting an account does not cancel store renewal.

Operational verification required

This local draft identifies the existing public support address. Mailbox handling, escalation ownership and response or completion periods must be qualified before publishing this release edition. No response-time guarantee is claimed here.
