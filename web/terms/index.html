Using Lovelies

Terms of use

Review your photos, respect the people pictured and share only what you are entitled to share.

Who can use Lovelies

You must be at least 13. If you are 13–17, a guardian must authorize the relevant purposes through the supported independent review process, and you must make your own separate purpose choices. Pending or withdrawn authority does not permit capture, upload or matching. Guardian authorization gives no access to your private photos. An account cannot switch itself from a restricted age band to adulthood; independent review and fresh purpose choices are required.

Use support, privacy and account-deletion controls even while eligibility is restricted. Regional requirements may limit availability.

Your responsibilities

Use Lovelies only for photos you are entitled to upload, process and share. Respect the people pictured and their privacy choices. Do not use the service for unlawful, abusive or objectionable content.

A possible face match is a suggestion, not a guarantee of identity or permission. Review every proposed audience and each person’s actual photo subset. A suggestion alone never grants photo access. Recipients must accept the relevant request before access can be granted.

Report an incorrect match or objectionable content and block an unwanted account through the app. If you cannot use the app, contact support.

Face authentication and privacy choices

Face comparison can be wrong. It is not a civil-identity or liveness check and does not attest that an image came from a camera sensor. Authentication, matching and processing uploaded photos have separate purpose controls.

Read the privacy notice for information about selected photos, profile selfies, providers, retention and account requests.

Optional store subscriptions

Optional subscriptions provide the original-download entitlement displayed in the app. The store presents the actual localized price, period and renewal terms before you purchase. Restore and manage purchases through the available app and store controls.

A pending, failed, expired or revoked purchase does not grant original access. Purchases, refunds, renewal and revocation follow verified store records and applicable store terms. Deleting Lovelies data does not cancel a store subscription; cancel renewal separately through the purchasing Apple or Google account.

The Apple standard licensed application terms remain linked on iOS alongside these product terms. Applicable consumer rights are preserved.

Copies shared outside Lovelies

Blocking, revocation or account deletion cannot withdraw photos already downloaded or shared outside the service. Consider the people involved and each selected subset before approving a share.

Questions and account requests

Lovelies is provided by IKANISA LTD. Email info@ikanisa.com for support. To request deletion without the app, follow the account-deletion instructions.
