Sharing takes a decision

A possible match is a suggestion. It never grants photo access by itself. The sender reviews each person’s exact photo subset, and the recipient must accept the relevant request before access can be granted.

Face authentication, matching and processing uploaded photos have separate controls. Store purchases and subscriptions remain subject to their verified store records.
