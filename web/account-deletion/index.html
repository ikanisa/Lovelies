Account and data

Delete your
Lovelies account

You can request deletion from the app or email support without installing it.

From the app

Open Settings → Privacy → Delete account. This route is available for an anonymous account before face registration too. Confirm the request and keep its displayed status or recovery information until it completes. You can return to the original request after restarting the app. No phone or email OTP is used.

If you cannot access the app

Email info@ikanisa.com with the subject Lovelies account deletion. This link opens your email app; you review and send the message yourself.

Email a deletion request ↗

Include only:

  • Your account ID or an existing support or privacy request ID, if known.
  • The approximate last-use date.
  • A return contact for support’s reply.
Keep sensitive material out of email

Do not send photos, selfies, face references or other biometric material, device keys, recovery backup secrets or payment-card details.

Support will explain a proportionate account-ownership check and how to track your request before changing private account data. An email is not proof of ownership and does not mean deletion is complete. Deletion is not automated from an unverified email.

What the deletion workflow covers

The workflow covers eligible account and face-profile data, consent records, private media and face references, gallery and sharing links, and related operational records. Current authority, ongoing writers and justified holds are checked. Only justified financial, security and minimal erasure evidence should remain.

Cleanup completion is shown only after the server confirms verified deletion. Copies previously downloaded or shared outside Lovelies cannot be withdrawn.

Completion and retention periods pending

The controller must approve and publish the active retention schedule, backup-expiry period and deletion completion periods before this new edition is released. The support mailbox and controlled request-handling workflow also need operational verification.

Cancel store renewal separately

Deleting your Lovelies account or data does not cancel an Apple or Google subscription. If you want to stop renewal, cancel it separately in the purchasing store account. Purchase, refund and renewal status follows the verified store records.
