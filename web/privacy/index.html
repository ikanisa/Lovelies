Face authentication is a separate choice

Lovelies starts with an anonymous account. If you choose face authentication, the app captures three profile selfies with ordinary front, left and right pose guidance. Those images are uploaded through encrypted transport to the private backend, which compares them before registering a face reference and upgrading the same account. Later sign-in compares a fresh signed capture with the registered reference.

There is no phone or email OTP, liveness test or civil-identity check. A face comparison does not prove that an image came from a camera sensor.

Face authentication, photo matching and processing uploaded photos have separate consent controls. Authentication consent does not itself enable the other purposes. Matching identifies possible recipients for review; it does not grant access. The sender reviews the exact photo–recipient selection and exclusions. The recipient must accept the relevant request before current access can be granted.

Information the service handles

  • Account and device identifiers, device public keys and signed capture proofs.
  • Uploaded selfies, derived face references, selected photos, reduced previews and captions you provide.
  • Consent and sharing records, profile and preference settings, support and safety cases, and privacy requests.
  • Store-purchase verification records, notification tokens if enabled, and security and operational audit records.

Device private signing keys stay in protected device or browser storage and are not uploaded. Face enrollment does not require access to your entire photo library. Photo import uses the photos or files you choose through system controls.

Processing, providers and private access

The selected backend is Supabase project ltqfplqevxztlswlzexn in EU West (Ireland), using private PostgreSQL records and private Storage. A separately authenticated private YuNet/SFace service processes the necessary image and reference bytes.

Publication input still required

The private inference service’s actual hosting operator and location must be recorded after deployment qualification. Add every additional processor that is actually enabled before final store disclosures.

Face-verification data are not sent to messaging providers for verification. Apple and Google process store purchases. APNs and FCM handle opted-in device-notification tokens and generic account-update messages; notification text does not contain private photos or face references.

Photos, captures and exports are private. Current authority is checked before short-lived preview, original or export access. An original requires the relevant access and a current verified production store entitlement. Consent changes, blocks and account, device and session status are checked by the server.

Lovelies cannot withdraw copies a recipient already saved or shared outside the service. Upload only content you are entitled to process and share.

Retention and verified deletion

Capture files are temporary processing evidence and are scheduled for cleanup after completion or expiry. Reference deletion, account deletion and retention work require actual object deletion followed by absence verification. Failed or uncertain cleanup stays pending.

Active face references, photos and operational records follow the configured retention policy and any justified hold. After deletion, only justified financial, security, legal-hold and minimal erasure evidence should be retained.

Retention schedule awaiting review

The approved active retention periods, backup-expiry period, deletion completion periods and necessity of retained records must be confirmed before publication. Current code defaults are implementation settings, not a finding that every record needs a particular legal retention period.

Your choices and requests

Use the app’s settings to review each purpose consent, stop matching, manage your face profile, block or report a person or content, request an export, or request account deletion. Deletion is available before face enrollment too. Sensitive account and face-profile changes use fresh authority and durable request/status handling.

A deletion request is tracked through review and verified cleanup. Sending a request does not mean deletion has completed. If you cannot use the app, follow the external account-deletion instructions or contact support. Support checks account ownership before disclosing or changing private data.

Never email selfies, face references, private keys, recovery secrets or payment-card details.

Subscriptions are managed through the purchasing Apple or Google account. Deleting Lovelies data does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel renewal separately in that store account if desired. Refund, renewal, expiry and revocation follow verified store records and applicable store terms.
