Face authentication is a separate choice

Lovelies starts with an anonymous account. If you choose face authentication, the app captures three profile selfies with ordinary front, left and right pose guidance. Those images are uploaded through encrypted transport to the private backend, which compares them before registering a face reference and upgrading the same account. Later sign-in compares a fresh signed capture with the registered reference.

There is no phone or email OTP, liveness test or civil-identity check. A face comparison does not prove that an image came from a camera sensor.

Face authentication, photo matching and processing uploaded photos have separate consent controls. Authentication consent does not itself enable the other purposes. Matching identifies possible recipients for review; it does not grant access. The sender reviews the exact photo–recipient selection and exclusions. The recipient must accept the relevant request before current access can be granted.

Information the service handles

  • Account and device identifiers, device public keys and signed capture proofs.
  • Uploaded selfies, derived face references, selected photos, reduced previews and captions you provide.
  • Consent and sharing records, age-band declarations, guardian requests and approvals, references to independent review where applicable, profile and preference settings, support and safety cases, and privacy requests.
  • Store-purchase verification records, notification tokens if enabled, and security and operational audit records.

Device private signing keys stay in protected device or browser storage and are not uploaded. Face enrollment does not require access to your entire photo library. Photo import uses the photos or files you choose through system controls. Selected photo files may contain embedded information, such as when and where the picture was taken.

Photos, captures and exports are private. Current authority is checked before short-lived preview, original or export access. Lovelies cannot withdraw copies a recipient already saved or shared outside the service. Upload only content you are entitled to process and share.

Retention and verified deletion

Capture files are temporary processing evidence and are scheduled for cleanup after completion or expiry. Reference deletion, account deletion and retention work require actual object deletion followed by absence verification. Failed or uncertain cleanup stays pending.

The selected release schedule keeps uploaded originals, previews and captions for 60 days from upload. Subscription or repeated viewing does not extend that deadline. Temporary sign-in and enrollment captures are queued for cleanup when the operation ends or expires, with verified removal targeted within one hour and a maximum normal recovery window of 24 hours. Current face references remain only while a valid, separately chosen purpose requires them; retired references are targeted for deletion within 24 hours.

Incomplete uploads expire after seven days, privacy exports after 24 hours, and app-managed temporary previews after two days. Minimal purpose and guardian receipts and privileged action or erasure evidence are selected for retention for up to 365 days after their relevant use ends; earlier valid erasure may shorten that period. General diagnostics are limited to seven days, minimized security events to 30 days, and ordinary support cases to 90 days after closure. Specific incident, financial or legal obligations may justify a narrower retained record with a recorded reason and review date; they do not justify retaining an unrestricted biometric archive.

Routine account erasure targets active-data removal within seven days of receipt and residual backup expiry by day 14, with an ordinary maximum of 30 days from receipt including backups. The selected rolling backup limit is seven days. A valid deletion request stays trackable through verified completion; an internal review delay does not restart the clock. Any justified exception is explained with its retained category and review route.

Selected schedule; operation checks pending

These are the selected release periods. Provider backup settings, monitored cleanup, native-cache expiry, restore suppression and support handling must be verified before this draft becomes effective. A configured default alone does not prove deletion.

Your choices and requests

Use the app’s settings to review each purpose consent, stop matching, manage your face profile, block or report a person or content, request an export, or request account deletion. Deletion is available before face enrollment too. Sensitive account and face-profile changes use fresh authority and durable request/status handling.

A deletion request is tracked through review and verified cleanup. Sending a request does not mean deletion has completed. The server’s completed status concerns active account erasure and its tracked private-file cleanup; backup-copy expiry requires a separate check. If you cannot use the app, follow the external account-deletion instructions or contact support. Support checks account ownership before disclosing or changing private data.

Never email selfies, face references, private keys, recovery secrets or payment-card details.

Subscriptions are managed through the purchasing Apple or Google account. Deleting Lovelies data does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel renewal separately in that store account if desired. Refund, renewal, expiry and revocation follow verified store records and applicable store terms.

Audience and markets

Lovelies is intended for people aged 13 and above. People under 13 cannot enroll or use photo processing. Ages 13–17 require a current guardian authorization for each relevant purpose, independently reviewed through the supported process, alongside the young person’s own separate choices. Unknown or pending eligibility stays restricted before capture and photo processing. A face comparison, adult declaration, relationship checkbox or permission to install the app alone does not prove parental responsibility. No face-age estimate or exact date of birth is used.

A guardian can withdraw their authorization without opening the young person’s photos. Approval grants no access to that library and no ability to impersonate the account or approve its sharing. Privacy, support, withdrawal and deletion remain accessible while eligibility is restricted. Approval is reviewed at least annually and when circumstances change. A transition to adulthood needs independent review and fresh purpose choices. Supported markets and the actual operated review process must be qualified before launch.
