**Name:** Lovelies

**Apple subtitle:** Your photos. Private sharing.

**Short description:** Find your photos. Review who receives them. Keep sharing private.

**Description:**

Lovelies brings your private photos and sharing decisions together.

Set up your profile with three guided selfies. Your private backend verifies the
captures before registering your profile. Return with a fresh face comparison;
there is no phone or email OTP. Authentication, matching and processing consent
remain separate choices.

Choose photos to upload, follow real transfer progress and return to interrupted
work. Browse your library, search, filter and view photos. Review suggested people,
select the exact photos for each recipient and exclude any pair before confirming.
A match suggestion alone never grants access; recipients approve their requests.

Manage your profile, device sessions and privacy choices. Block or report unwanted
content or an incorrect match, contact support, request an export or request account
deletion. Optional store subscriptions provide the verified original-download
entitlement shown in the app. The store displays the current price and renewal
terms before purchase, with restore/manage controls available.

Use only content you have permission to process and share. Face comparison can be
wrong; review every selection. It does not verify civil identity or liveness.
