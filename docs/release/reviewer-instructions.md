# Reviewer workflow for the native replacement

Draft; validate on the exact signed candidate and keep the qualified backend
available before submission. Use the existing Lovelies/Memories store records,
not a new listing. Explain the native rewrite and selected Supabase backend in
review notes. No real legacy customers, photos or subscriptions are being imported.

Start a fresh anonymous account. Read the separate face-authentication purpose
notice and register with your own three guided profile captures. The backend
verifies them before upgrading that same account. There is no OTP or liveness
challenge. Subsequent sign-in compares a fresh signed capture with that registered
reference; a reviewer's selfie cannot unlock someone else's enrolled account.

Browse the empty/library states, choose permitted photos from system Photos/Files,
confirm upload choices and observe transfer vs matching progress. For sharing,
use two independently enrolled consenting test participants and images they have
permission to upload. Review the exact photo/recipient pairs, exclude a pair,
confirm, then accept the request from the intended recipient before viewing it.
A match proposal alone must grant no access. Reviewers can explore single-account
upload/privacy behavior without access to another person's face profile.

Use store sandbox testing for product loading, cancel/pending, purchase, restore,
expiry and revocation. Production-original access must remain unavailable from a
sandbox-only entitlement. Display the actual store-localized price/period and
renewal/manage controls. Provide the exact product and qualified sandbox setup in
the final review notes without exposing private provider keys or real-user tokens.

Exercise logout/fresh sign-in, a saved interrupted upload, block/report, support,
privacy export and anonymous/registered deletion request status. An authorized
independent operator reviews controlled requests; describe the operated response
arrangements and avoid promising instantaneous deletion before verified cleanup.

The final access notes must include tested steps, supported devices, working
privacy/support/deletion links and an operated reviewer support contact. Confirm
that own-profile enrollment is workable for the reviewing team. If they require a
reusable account or demo alternative, agree an authorized fully featured method
without a production grant bypass or pre-enrolled biometric impersonation. No
reviewer exception, private fixture account or review approval is claimed here.
