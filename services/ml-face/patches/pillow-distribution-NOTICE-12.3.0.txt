Pillow FriBiDi loader shim
-------------------------

fribidi-shim as LGPL-2.1-or-later. It is distinct from the optional full
FriBiDi library that the shim can load dynamically. Pillow's original setup.py
adds the shim and vendored RAQM sources to PIL._imagingft when the corresponding
vendor build settings are enabled. The complete original Pillow 12.3.0 source
