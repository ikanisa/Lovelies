Lovelies local glibc 2.41 maintenance and thread runtime build

Base: Debian glibc 2.41-12+deb13u4, with its exact Debian quilt series.
Modifications: the exact upstream release/2.41 maintenance commits identified
in glibc-backport-manifest.json, covering ten 2026 CVE IDs with four required
ns_print prerequisites and three additional vendor regression test commits.
Two local source patches add separate guarded TLS/TCB storage for new pthreads,
fresh random managed-stack mappings, and compiled exclusion of reusable managed
stacks, per-thread malloc tcache and nonmain malloc arenas. The package identity
is 2.41-12+deb13u4+lovelies2; its source receipt covers 80 Debian, 17 upstream and
two local patches. Required local fixture runs and vendor NPTL/malloc results
are bound to the measured libc, loader, fixture sources and output receipts.

This is a modified glibc 2.41 build. It is not an official Debian release or
an unmodified upstream distribution. Seven older glibc advisory IDs remain
unfixed and must remain in the release findings.
Local thread-path exclusions are scoped to this service build. They do not
change the vendor's unfixed advisory status or establish universal ASLR entropy.
Ordinary main-arena allocation reuse and unrelated memory-corruption paths
remain outside that exclusion claim. Public ABI symbol comparisons alone do
not establish private TLS/stack compatibility; measured native fixtures are
required before packaging.

The package contains the original licenses, Debian copyright, exact source
archives and descriptor, modification patches, source preparation and build
recipes, manifests and measured build/test/API evidence. The original glibc
LGPL and applicable individual source notices apply. No blanket security,
deployment, or independent acceptance claim is made.
