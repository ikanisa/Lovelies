HEIF runtime packaging notice
24 October 2026 — prepared source candidate

The still-image runtime retains shared HEIC/HEIF decoder components. This
source preparation does not broaden the admitted Lovelies private-inference
input contract or require HEIC/HEIF encoding. The Dockerfile builds the locked
pillow-heif source
distribution against shared Debian libheif libraries, instead of installing
the upstream wheel that bundles native libraries and a GPL x265 encoder.
