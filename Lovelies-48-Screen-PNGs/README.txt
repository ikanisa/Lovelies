LOVELIES — 48 INDIVIDUAL APP SCREENS

The 48 PNG files are exact individual-screen crops from the four latest
Lovelies dark-theme storyboard images. They retain the original photography,
branding, copy and UI. They are not reconstructed editable UI components.

The PowerPoint contains one image per portrait page (48 pages), in original
numbered order, ready for Canva import. The images use contain sizing, so
all source proportions are preserved and no screen content is cropped off.
The original mockups have varying aspect ratios; dark padding is intentional.

Pages 01–12: Setup, permissions, face enrollment, home and viewer.
Pages 13–24: Library, discovery, activity and uploads.
Pages 25–36: Matching, photo/person selections, sharing and report.
Pages 37–48: Profile, privacy, settings, help, account and empty/error states.

Resolution: These are native-resolution crops from image-generated boards.
They are not higher-resolution redraws; enlarging them cannot add UI detail.

See screen-index.csv and screen-manifest.json for names, numbering and source
traceability. No fonts are included.
